A Twitch chat app for Android and iOS, inspired by DankChat.

- Twitch, BTTV, FFZ and 7TV emotes, novel emote cache system
- Saveable threads that don't decay hard
- Multiple channels in swipeable tabs, multiple accounts
- Pinned messages, polls, predictions and hype trains above chat
- Custom highlights and mention notifications
- (Almost) full mod view
- Experimental liquid glass
- Features you'd expect in other chat clients
- Lots of customizability!
