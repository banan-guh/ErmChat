Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

ErmChat has no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics. I don't use the app for analytics or advertising.

Services the app talks to

To work, the app connects directly to:

  • Twitch, for chat, login and the stream player
  • 7TV, BTTV and FFZ, for emotes and badges
  • recent-messages (robotty.de, zneix.eu), for chat from before you opened a channel
  • Chatterino, DankChat, Chatsen and Limerino, for their badges
  • kappa.lol, only when you upload a picture

These services see your IP address, and some see which channels you open. Each has its own privacy policy.

Optional iOS push notifications

If you turn on iOS push notifications, the app sends a push service your Twitch access token, Apple device token, joined channel names, display name, keyword and user notification rules, blocked sender logins and mute deadline. The service uses this data only to match mentions and deliver notifications through Apple Push Notification service. The app asks the service to remove its registration when you turn push off, sign out or switch accounts.

Notifications can show the sender and message on your lock screen. iOS notification settings control whether that content is visible. This data is not used for analytics or advertising.

Bug reports

Nothing is sent unless you send a report. A report includes what you write and, if "Include diagnostics" is on, your app version, device, settings and a recent performance log, which you can read before sending. Reports are posted publicly as issues on GitHub (github.com/banan-guh/ErmChat/issues) and in the ErmChat Discord, along with your Twitch username. If you want a report removed, email me.
